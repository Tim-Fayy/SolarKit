What problem does Solar Kit solve?

Many city-dwellers want to reduce electricity bills and their carbon footprint but lack access to roofs or permanent installations. Solar Kit gives renters and apartment owners a simple, reversible way to harvest solar energy from balconies — powering lights, charging devices, or top-up batteries for small appliances.

Who is it for?

People living in apartments with balcony space, tiny-home owners, students, and eco-conscious city residents who want an affordable, portable solar option without drilling or roof access.

Quick specs

  • Peak power: up to 200W per panel (nominal 160W typical)
  • Mounting: adjustable clamp system for common rail sizes
  • Outputs: USB-A/C (fast-charge), 12V output, optional inverter for AC appliances
  • Material: tempered glass solar cells, anodized aluminum frame
